I've noticed an increase in Claude-generated artefacts that all look the same. People are designing websites, graphics, and infographics that all look like Claude-branded assets, when they're meant to be unique, authored assets from that individual. Not from Anthropic themselves.

## Same palette, same type, same layout

It's the palette. It's the typography. It's the layouts. A lot of the time it's just a rehashing. The content could be completely different. The points could be completely clear. They're all in the same style of infographic, and it makes you tune out immediately and ignore the graphic itself.

## We've been here before

WordArt, like Comic Sans, cheapened words and design. It was used across a broad spectrum of industries, from takeaway flyers to quite serious commercial presentations, and it cheapened the point being landed because everything felt the same. There was no unique angle to make people really pay attention to the content.

## The problem is the defaults

I'm very up for using AI to amplify your thoughts and ideas. The real challenge comes when you use it to author something and take the default design system.

## What to do about it

Your approach depends on your technical ability.

If you're technical, consider building your own design system to base these things off. Give more steer on the layouts you want, to really land the points. Come up with your own visual style. Try not to use the defaults as a foundation, because ultimately you're going to end up with a very similar palette and typography set.

If you're not technical, I'm honestly not sure what the best route is. If you can't build out a design system, you probably can't design a unique look and feel in the first place. Maybe pay a designer to help you build out that initial design system. Using humans to build something different is still a really great thing to do. Try not to outsource absolutely everything to AI.

## Beyond design

I suspect the same thing is happening in code. That's a post for another day.

## Differentiate yourself

Take a real look at how you differentiate yourself in a sea of noise. Make sure what you're building is still unique, and that you're still offering something different.
